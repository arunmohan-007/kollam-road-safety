Data and methodology

Crash data is grouped by FIR and filtered by accident date. Casualties include workbook crashes with no usable map location when filters allow. Road-class filters use the GIS match. Black-spot cause summaries use the severe crashes assigned to each original 500 m stretch. Year, cause and crash severity filters show black spots with at least one matching contributing crash and keep the published three-year rank.

Road matching uses up to 20 m on either side of the mapped road. MoRTH qualifies a 500 m stretch with five or more fatal or grievous-injury crashes or ten or more people killed in three years. NATPAC CSI equals seven times fatal crashes plus three times grievous-injury crashes.
